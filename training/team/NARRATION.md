# The Switch — team training · narration script (chapters 1, 2, 6, 7)

Calm narrator, generic (no names). Times are when each line starts, matched to the recording. Captions use the same words (the `.vtt` file beside each video).

---

## Chapter 1 · Signing in (desktop, 0:28)

- **0:02** Welcome to The Switch admin. Let's start by signing in.
- **0:04** On switchme.io, Sign in is the last item in the top bar.
- **0:07** You can continue with Google, or use your email and password.
- **0:11** Type your email, then your password. It shows as dots.
- **0:18** Press Sign in, and the admin opens on Overview.
- **0:20** Your account is at the top right. That's also where you sign out.
- **0:23** The admin menu runs down the left side. Everything you need starts here.

## Chapter 1 · Signing in (phone, 0:26)

- **0:02** On your phone, it works the same way.
- **0:04** Tap the menu at the top right. Sign in is at the bottom.
- **0:07** Continue with Google, or type your email and password.
- **0:16** Tap Sign in, and the admin opens.
- **0:18** The menu button at the top left opens the whole admin menu.
- **0:21** And the bar along the bottom has four shortcuts: Overview, Needs attention, Brand check and Brands.

## Chapter 2 · Finding your way (desktop, 1:26)

- **0:02** This is Overview. The menu on the left takes you everywhere.
- **0:04** Overview is the health of The Switch at a glance.
- **0:06** Analytics has The Switch's own numbers: visits, switches, and money moved to women-owned businesses.
- **0:09** The Switch is where most of the work happens: brands, products, brand checks and service providers.
- **0:11** The Switch Society opens the Society site.
- **0:14** Needs attention lists everything waiting on a person. The badge shows how many.
- **0:16** Switch outreach is where you contact brands.
- **0:19** Places holds local businesses that have applied.
- **0:21** Growth tracks growth work and the brands each person brought in.
- **0:24** And Lists shows products, types and brands as searchable lists.
- **0:32** Let's look at Overview.
- **0:33** Auto-publish shows whether ready products go live by themselves, and what published today.
- **0:36** Some controls are greyed out. The reason is always written beside them. These belong to the owner.
- **0:40** Out of stock shows products hidden because the store sold out. They come back on their own when restocked.
- **0:42** The funnel follows people from a visit to their first switch.
- **0:45** And launch sign-ups shows who joined, and from where.
- **0:49** Further down are affiliate sales, held clicks, and today's spend.
- **1:01** Now, Needs attention. This is your to-do list.
- **1:02** The headline count matches the badge in the menu.
- **1:05** Shopper requests are things shoppers asked for that we don't have yet.
- **1:08** Every card has one main button that takes you straight to the fix.
- **1:11** Brands to review are drafts that are ready to publish.
- **1:14** Products to approve are ready but not yet live.
- **1:18** Problems are live brands missing something, like a logo.
- **1:22** Housekeeping is for reference only. Nothing there needs you.

## Chapter 6 · Adding a brand (desktop, 1:55)

- **0:02** Now let's add a new women-owned brand.
- **0:04** Open The Switch, then the Add a brand tab.
- **0:06** There are five steps: Brand, Owner and proof, Shopping, Products, and Review and publish.
- **0:08** Start with the brand's website.
- **0:13** Press Fetch. It reads her store, finds her products, and checks for an affiliate program.
- **0:17** Here it found four products. They'll wait at step four.
- **0:20** The name, description and logo were filled in for you. Always check them.
- **0:26** Add a one-line story, in our own words.
- **0:30** Your work saves as you type. Nothing is public until you publish.
- **0:35** Step two, Owner and proof. This is the most important step.
- **0:37** Enter the owner's full name, first and last.
- **0:40** Then the share that's women-owned.
- **0:43** The date you checked her ownership.
- **0:46** The kind of evidence you found.
- **0:48** And the link where you saw it. Use a real link or a named document, never just a note.
- **0:52** Where she's based.
- **0:54** And the founder's email, so she can be sent the certification invitation.
- **0:59** Step three, Shopping. Choose how clicks are paid. Direct means no affiliate network yet.
- **1:02** Payout details are greyed out. Those are the owner's.
- **1:06** And where her products ship from.
- **1:10** Step four, Products. Here are the four products Fetch found.
- **1:15** Each one gets a product type and a short why line. Ready means it can publish.
- **1:20** Under the table, choose her category. You can use the suggestion.
- **1:27** Step five, Review and publish. Everything is in one list.
- **1:29** Green means done. Grey means optional.
- **1:36** Choose to send the owner certification invitation after publishing. That's the usual choice.
- **1:40** Publish checks everything one more time, then her products go live.
- **1:43** And that's it.
- **1:47** Her products are published, and the brand now shows as live. In this demo, nothing really went live.

## Chapter 7 · Adding a service provider (desktop, 1:12)

- **0:02** Service providers are women-owned businesses that offer a service, verified just like brands.
- **0:05** Open The Switch, then the Providers tab.
- **0:07** The list shows each provider, whether she's a draft or live, and whether she's verified.
- **0:12** Press Add provider.
- **0:14** Type the business name.
- **0:17** Choose the kind of service.
- **0:19** List what she does.
- **0:25** Add her story, using only what she wrote or approved.
- **0:31** Then where she works: her city and state.
- **0:37** And her website, so shoppers can reach her.
- **0:41** Ownership details are for admins only, and they're needed to publish.
- **0:42** Published stays greyed out until everything is in. The line under it tells you exactly what's missing.
- **0:46** Enter the owner's full name.
- **0:49** The date you checked.
- **0:52** The kind of evidence.
- **0:54** And where you saw it.
- **0:57** Now every requirement is met, so Published can be ticked.
- **1:03** Press Add.
- **1:05** And she's in the list, live and verified. In this demo, nothing really went live.
